        Data Structures Summary

        • Doubly Linked List: next and previous playlist navigation.
        • Hash Map: fast lookup of songs by unique ID.
        • Queue: first-in, first-out Up Next playback.
        • Stack: last-in, first-out history with a limit of 10.
        • Binary Search Tree: song titles and prefix search.
        • Undirected Graph: artist connections and two-hop recommendations.
